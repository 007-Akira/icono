# Brag Plan: ICONO

## Product and angle
ICONO Beauty World & Makeover Studio is a founder-led salon in Karicode, Kollam. The film is a quiet editorial portrait: real imagery at cinematic scale, services as photographic spreads, founder as the human centre, and the real WhatsApp appointment flow as the close.

## Planning rubric
1. App: an editorial salon website covering hair, skin, bridal, grooming, gallery, founder story, and WhatsApp enquiries.
2. Strongest line: “Beauty, shaped with intention.”
3. Visual hook: split editorial hero and asymmetrical portfolio mosaic.
4. Actual UI: hero, service triptych, gallery, founder, appointment picker, WhatsApp CTA.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 36s, because the requested 30–40s duration and luxury pacing need long photographic holds.
6. Tone: polished; premium beauty/editorial commercial with cinematic scale and restraint.
7. Audio: low warm bed, three subtle tactile accents.
8. Share caption: ICONO — beauty, shaped with intention. A founder-led beauty world and makeover studio in Karicode, Kollam.
9. Flow: discover the work → select a service → continue on WhatsApp.

## Format and identity
- Landscape 1920×1080, 36 seconds, no voiceover
- Floral #FFFBF4; smoke #11120D; olive #565449; bone #D8CFBC
- Playfair Display with Inter
- Avoid SaaS cards, metrics, hype, fast zooms, and exaggerated claims

## Storyboard
1. **Intention — 6s.** Split hero, studio eyebrow, and existing tagline. Slow opposing push-ins.
2. **Signature Services — 6s.** Hair, Skin & Facial, Bridal & Makeup reveal as an editorial triptych.
3. **Selected Work — 6.5s.** Four real portfolio images form a full-bleed moving contact sheet.
4. **Founder / Studio — 6s.** Grayscale Ms. Revathy portrait with “A story rooted in care.”
5. **Appointment — 5.5s.** Service picker selects Bridal & Makeup; CTA resolves to Continue to WhatsApp.
6. **ICONO — 6s.** Existing logo, tagline, Karicode · Kollam, and WhatsApp close.

## Audio
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.18 volume
- Preset: 109.96 BPM; optional anchors around 8.74s, 17.47s, 22.93s
- SFX: one warm reveal, one quiet click, one soft logo accent
- No audio-reactive pulsing; stillness is part of the premium direction

The user’s explicit 30–40 second target overrides BRAG’s usual 15–25 second default. The extra duration is used for photographic breathing room, not additional copy.
