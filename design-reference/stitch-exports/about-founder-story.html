Expertise

Ms. Latha

With over two decades of dedicated practice, Ms. Latha approaches cosmetology as an architectural discipline. It is about understanding the underlying structure of individual beauty and enhancing it with precision. Her approach rejects the superficial mask, favoring treatments that restore vitality from within.

Certified by leading international cosmetic boards, she continuously integrates advanced dermatological insights with tactile, restorative therapies, ensuring every protocol at Icono meets the highest standards of efficacy and safety.
